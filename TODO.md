# main mechanics

- Woodcutting
- Fishing

## Inner Mechanics

- Woodcutting:
  - damage tree per click
  - click damage is determined by the axe you have equipped
  - Different trees available at different levels
  - higher level trees take more clicks to chop down
    - maybe have later talents to allow multiple logs per tree

- Fishing:
  - Fishing takes time (loading bar fills up)
  - time to fish determined by tier of fishing rod
  - Different fish available at different levels.
    - higher level fish take more time to catch
    - maybe have later talents to allow multiple fish per catch

## Services

- Each skill will have its own service for how the environment works.
- Service will be a class.
